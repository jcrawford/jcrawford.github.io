## Notes

This dandelion mead is ready to drink immediately after bottling — no bottle conditioning required.

The fermentation was remarkably straightforward. Red Star Premier Côte is a reliable yeast that ferments cleanly and flocculates well, leaving behind a mead that's bright and easy to drink.

**About the photos:** I don't have many images of this brew because I threw it together quickly once I had the flowers deconstructed. The process was impromptu — once the petals were separated from the stems, everything moved fast.

&nbsp;

## Tasting Notes

The finished mead pours a pale golden color with excellent clarity. The nose carries distinct floral notes — partly from the Ambrosia honey, partly from the dandelion tea. On the palate, it's lightly sweet with a dry finish. The dandelion contributes an herbal, almost tea-like quality that makes this mead feel both rustic and refined. It's refreshing served slightly chilled.
