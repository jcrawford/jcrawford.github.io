## Notes

This is a planned batch, not yet started. The goal is a strong, complex apple bochet built on a base of 100% apple juice, caramelized bochet honey, and fresh sliced apples. The high sugar load from the honey and sugar should push the finished ABV above 15%, so patience during aging will be important.

**Bochet honey is the wildcard here.** Caramelizing the honey drives off some of its fermentable sugars and adds toasted, burnt-sugar, and dark-fruit notes. Watch the pot carefully — honey can go from caramelized to scorched quickly.

**Apple timing:** Adding the fruit in primary will extract the most flavor and fermentable sugars. A small secondary apple addition is worth considering if the flavor is too subtle after primary.
