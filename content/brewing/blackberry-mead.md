## Lessons for Next Time

This batch taught me several things I will do differently next time:

- **Take an original gravity reading.** Without an OG I cannot calculate ABV, which is useful information for labeling and sharing the recipe.
- **Measure back-sweetening additions.** Eyeballing the honey left me with no reproducible data. Next batch I will weigh the honey before and after adding it.
- **Keep better yeast notes.** I recorded Red Star Premier Cote Des Blancs for this batch, but on future batches I will also write down the exact nutrient schedule and any other additions.
